    Roadmap to the Platinum

    Platinum facts: about 200 hours · difficulty 4/10 · 1 playthrough · 0 online trophies · no DLC or multiplayer needed · no mission replay · 15 manual save slots · cheats disable trophies and saving.

    1. Step 1 — Play the story, and mind the one missable trophy

      Play Chapters 1–5 in order and earn the 12 story trophies as they come. Warning: in Chapter 1, after the mission “Down to Earth”, Lucia is in the kitchen in the morning and says she wants to meditate — follow her to the balcony of Jason’s house and choose Accept. That first meditation is the only trophy that may be missable: Positive Mental Attitude needs all 18 meditation spots, and this balcony spot may only exist in Chapter 1. Do it the first morning it is offered, then meditate at every other spot with both Jason and Lucia.

    2. Step 2 — 100% Completion Checklist

      After the story, open Touchpad > Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. A story-only run lands at only about 60% true completion. The checklist needs: 100 optional missions, 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 300 stunts, 70 of 80 challenges, 15 of 18 meditations, plus every robbery, bank job, drone race and shooting range. This is the long grind — budget around 100 hours after the story — and it unlocks the gold trophy A Long Road.

    3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and the cleanup

      Finish the stranger questlines (only certain strands count toward the eight for Strangers & Freaks — see its guide), then sweep up the remaining collectibles, activities and robberies. When the 54th trophy pops, the platinum Vice Got a Price! follows automatically.
